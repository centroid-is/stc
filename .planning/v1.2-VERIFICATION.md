## Gaps

No FAIL. The two PARTIAL items are documented pending-user hand-offs, so frontmatter status is `passed` per the team lead's rule. Neither criterion is proven until the user acts.

1. **Phase 29 SC2, TF6100 divergence guard is inert.** Until a capture is committed, CI cannot detect divergence from the real TF6100 server.
   ```
   go test ./tests -run TestTF6100Diff -v -count=1
   # --- SKIP: TestTF6100Diff: no real TF6100 snapshot; capture with:
   #   stc opcua snapshot opc.tcp://<plc>:4840 --out tests/opcua_golden/st301_real.json
   ```
   Close it by capturing from the plant PLC and committing the file, or by setting `STC_TF6100_SNAPSHOT`. Then rerun with `STC_SILD_DIR` set.
2. **Phase 29 SC3, Flutter HMI live session not demonstrated.** Follow docs/OPCUA.md "Connect the Flutter HMI" against:
   ```
   stc serve "ST301 solution.tsproj" --io "IO List from ethercat/ST301/Device*.xml" --opcua :4840
   ```
   Confirm that the drive, sensor and conveyor structs show live values and enums such as `rdy(2)`.

- **ST301 scan cost of about 8 ms.** A 1 ms realtime task cannot be paced on the host. Serve at 10 ms showed 65 overruns in 735 cycles.
- **Phase 21 deferred.** These cover PVOID typing, the double-quoted `qualified_only` assumption needing user confirmation, method-statement-with-args SEMA024, external read of FB internal VAR, LEN typing, mixed signed/unsigned promotion, and Baader IO-generated types.

## Warnings (non-blocking)

- **The validator rejects the project and globs.** `stc ecat validate` does not accept the `.tsproj` or a `Device*.xml` glob. Passing the tsproj parses it as ST and reports P002, and a quoted glob reports `open .../Device*.xml: no such file or directory`. Running it on the real ST301 project needs the flattened probe `.st` files. sim, test and serve accept both forms.
- **gofmt drift.** `gofmt -l` lists cmd/stc/main.go, pkg/ast/test_nodes.go, pkg/ast/trivia.go, pkg/checker/vendor.go, pkg/incremental/filecache.go and several test files.
